A Practical Guide to Planning Your Interior Design Project
Planning an interior project involves more than choosing colours and furniture. It requires a clear understanding of the space, the intended budget, the materials to be used, and the order in which different tasks will be completed.
Whether you are updating one room or planning interiors for an entire property, having a structured approach can make decisions easier and help avoid unnecessary changes during execution.
Steps to Take Before Starting
Define your requirements: List the spaces you want to design and the features you need in each one.
Set a working budget: Account for materials, furniture, lighting, installation, and an appropriate contingency.
Finalise the layout: Confirm furniture positions, storage, electrical points, and circulation before work begins.
Compare materials: Review appearance, durability, maintenance, and cost.
Clarify the project scope: Understand what is included, the expected timeline, and how changes will be managed.
Communication is important throughout the project. Discussing design decisions early can help ensure that the finished space reflects your preferences and practical needs.
It is also useful to prioritise long-term usability over short-lived trends. A balanced interior plan should look appealing while supporting the way the space will be used every day.
